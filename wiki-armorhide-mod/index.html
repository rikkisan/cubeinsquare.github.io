ArmorHide: hide or fade the armor on players

ArmorHide is a client-side Fabric mod from Cube in Square that draws player armor translucent or not at all, so skins and costumes stay readable. Version 1.0.3 runs on Minecraft Java 1.21.11. It changes how armor looks on your screen, not the armor itself.

What it does

  • Fades armor to a transparency you choose, or hides it.
  • Treats your own player model and other players separately: hide armor on yourself, on others, or on both.
  • Works piece by piece: helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots each have their own switch.
  • Leaves mobs and armor stands alone. Only players are affected.
  • If another player damages you, hidden armor becomes visible for 10 seconds.

Because the mod is client-side, it changes only what your game draws. Players without it see armor as usual, and the armor stays equipped: it is the picture that changes, not the item.

Install

  1. Install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 1.21.11 with the installer from fabricmc.net, or create a Fabric 1.21.11 instance in a launcher that supports it.
  2. Download ArmorHide 1.0.3 from the Versions tab on Modrinth. Modrinth lists no required dependencies for it.
  3. Put the .jar file into the mods folder: .minecraft/mods for the official launcher, or the instance’s own mods folder.
  4. Start the game with the Fabric 1.21.11 profile. Servers you join do not need the mod.

Settings

The settings screen in the Modrinth gallery has these options:

Hide on selfHides armor on your own player model.
Hide on othersHides armor on other players.
TransparencyHow strongly hidden armor fades. A slider; the screenshot shows 50%.
Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, BootsWhether each piece is hidden.

The mod also adds a key binding, H by default. Change it under Options → Controls → Key Binds like any other key.

Limits

  • Fabric only, and only Minecraft 1.21.11: version 1.0.3 is the single file on Modrinth.
  • It changes your screen only. For a scene to look the same to everyone, every player needs the mod with similar settings.
  • Mobs and armor stands always show their armor.
  • A hit from another player brings hidden armor back for 10 seconds unless you switch off "Reveal after player hit".

Why we made it

ArmorHide came out of the Cube in Square roleplay server. Heavy armor kept covering the skins and costumes that tell other players who a character is, and asking everyone to take armor off in a dangerous world was not a real answer. Hiding it on screen keeps both the protection and a readable scene. If you are drawing those skins, the skin editor and its guide are on this site.

FAQ

Does the server need ArmorHide?

No. Modrinth marks it as a client-side mod: install it in your own game only.

Will other players see my armor hidden?

Only if they run ArmorHide themselves with "Hide on others" switched on. Without the mod they see your armor normally.

Does it hide armor on mobs or armor stands?

No, it works on players only.

Is there a build for Forge, NeoForge or another Minecraft version?

Not in version 1.0.3. Modrinth lists one file, for Fabric on 1.21.11.
