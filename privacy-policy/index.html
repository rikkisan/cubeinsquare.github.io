Privacy Policy

This page explains what data Cube in Square processes, what it keeps in your browser, how Google Analytics and Google AdSense work on the site, and how to change your consent choice.

Last updated: October 24, 2026.

In short

Local processing

The main generators use your PNG files and settings directly in the browser. Under normal tool usage, the site does not upload those files to its own server.

Google services

Google Analytics measures which pages and tools are used, and Google AdSense shows ads. Both receive technical request details from your browser.

Your choice

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your consent; elsewhere, the site's own banner does. "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page changes your answer.

Privacy contact

If you have a question about privacy, site operations, or advertising, you can contact us on Discord: kvaasssssssss.

1. Data processed by the site

Cube in Square works as a browser-based toolkit for Minecraft resource packs and related server workflows. Texture files uploaded to the generator are processed locally in your browser to build a ZIP archive. The site does not require registration and does not ask for your name, address, phone number, or similar direct personal details.

The site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub receives the usual request details, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. GitHub states that it logs the IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages sites for security purposes; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The site has no server-side code or database of its own.

2. Your consent choice

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent message from Google appears on your first visit. It comes from Google's consent management platform, which is registered with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. The message names the purposes and the advertising partners, lets you consent or refuse, and lets you choose purposes and partners one by one under "Manage options". Google keeps your choice in cookies on this site.

Everywhere else, the site shows its own banner with "Accept" and "Decline", and saves your answer in your browser:

  • Accept: Google Analytics may set its cookies, and Google AdSense may show personalised ads.
  • Decline: Google Analytics sets no cookies, and on the pages you open afterwards the site asks Google AdSense for non-personalised ads.
  • Until you answer, Google Analytics sets no cookies, while AdSense may show personalised ads.

If Google's message cannot load, for example because an ad blocker stops it, the site's own banner is shown instead, in any region.

To change your answer, use "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page. It opens Google's message in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and the site's banner elsewhere.

3. Google Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4, a Google service, to see which pages and tools are used and to improve them. It measures:

  • page views: the page address, title and language;
  • actions in the tools, such as copying a result, downloading a file, loading an example, switching between the form and the node editor, adding a potion effect or uploading an image. These events describe the action (for example the kind of output, the selected Minecraft version or the size of an image in pixels), not its content: your files, textures, commands and JSON are not sent;
  • clicks on links between a tool and its guide;
  • the text of an example when you copy it from a guide;
  • what you type into the site search;
  • your answer in the consent banner or in Google's message.

With your consent, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_HDJD70TN5K) to recognise repeat visits. Without consent it sets no cookies; the Google tag still sends these events without cookie identifiers, which Google uses for aggregated and modelled reports. Google receives your IP address with each request, as any server does, and states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Event data is kept for 14 months.

4. Cookies and browser storage

The site's own scripts set no cookies. They keep the following values in your browser's storage, where they stay on your device; the site has no server that reads them:

  • cube_cookie_consent: your answer in the site's consent banner;
  • cubeinsquare-design-mode and cubeinsquare-readable-font: the design and font you picked;
  • cube-nodes-layout:…: where you placed the nodes in a tool's node editor;
  • cubeinsquare-custom-item-catalog-v1: the items you saved in the custom item catalog;
  • cube_pending_link_click (session storage): a click on a link to a tool or a guide, kept until the next page reports it to Google Analytics and deleted when you close the tab.

Google sets its own cookies: Google Analytics with your consent (section 3), Google's consent message to remember your choice (section 2), and Google AdSense for ads (section 5). You can delete cookies and site data in your browser at any time; the site then asks for your choice again.

5. Google AdSense and ads

The site uses Google AdSense, which may show ads on its pages. Google and its partners may use cookies, advertising identifiers and similar technologies to show ads, measure performance, prevent fraud and comply with advertising policies.

Personalised ads are chosen from visits to this and other websites. Non-personalised ads are chosen from the content of the page and your approximate location, but Google still uses cookies or similar technologies for them, to limit how often an ad is shown, for aggregated reporting and against fraud. Where there is no consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google may show limited ads, which do not use personal data for personalisation.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, ads follow your choice in Google's message. Elsewhere they follow the site's banner (section 2). You can also turn off personalised ads in Google's ad settings.

6. External resources

To run the interface and Minecraft gallery, the site may load data or libraries from third-party sources, including JavaScript CDNs and public repositories with Minecraft-related data. When those requests happen, your browser may send technical request details to those services.

The site may also link to external websites. We do not control their privacy policies, so it is a good idea to review their own terms separately.

7. User files

Textures selected in the generator are used for local resource-pack creation in the browser. The site does not upload those PNG files to a Cube in Square server. The final ZIP is created on your device and downloaded through the browser.

8. Children and younger users

The site is about Minecraft resource packs and may be interesting to teenagers. We do not intentionally collect children's personal data. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child shared personal information through the site, contact the site owner so the issue can be reviewed and handled if such data was actually received.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when site features, advertising services, or legal requirements change. The current version is always published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy, advertising, or site-operation questions, you can contact us on Discord: kvaasssssssss. Basic project information is also available on the Project page.
